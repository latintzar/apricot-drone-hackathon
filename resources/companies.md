## More paths to explore

These are discovery links. Read the current product, team and careers pages to establish fit and availability.

- [Fly4Future](https://fly4future.com/): Czech drone engineering and research.
- [Fadron](https://fadron.com/): Bulgarian drone engineering.
- [Aviosense](https://www.aviosense.tech/): sensing and aviation systems.
- [D Aerospace](https://d-aerospace.com/): Bulgarian unmanned aircraft.
- [Smart Farm Robotix](https://smartfarmrobotix.eu/): field robotics for agriculture.
- [Skydio](https://www.skydio.com/): autonomous flight and inspection.
- [Boston Dynamics](https://bostondynamics.com/): robots, movement and real-world deployment.
- [Physical Intelligence](https://www.physicalintelligence.company/): research on learning for robots.

Your interests can also lead toward [Oxford Nanopore](https://nanoporetech.com/) in biological measurement or [CMR Surgical](https://cmrsurgical.com/) in surgical robotics. A useful device combines software, sensors, physical design and people using it.

## Find more companies yourself

Browse the portfolios of [Eleven](https://www.11.vc/), [LAUNCHub](https://www.launchub.vc/), [Vitosha](https://www.vitosha.vc/) and [Neo](https://www.neoventures.net/). Open companies whose products interest you. Use the [YC company directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) and [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/) to explore further.

A funding announcement is a reason to investigate a team. A current product, relevant work, good mentorship and a suitable role tell you more about whether to join it.

## Research and open-source work

- [INSAIT SURF](https://insait.ai/surf/): check the next published undergraduate research programme.
- [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/): explore projects and meet their communities ahead of applications.
- [ArduPilot](https://github.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot) and [PX4](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot): start with instructions, reproducible bugs or a small change agreed with a maintainer.

Use a private outreach sheet: company, product page, person, question, project evidence, date contacted, reply and next action. Check enrolment, location, work-authorisation and language requirements for each role you pursue.
